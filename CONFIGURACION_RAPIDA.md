```js
whatsapp: '584125477119'
whatsappDisplay: '0412-5477119'
emails: ['somosnexusstudio@gmail.com','contactooliverlugo@gmail.com']
```

- `assets/brand/nexus-icon.webp`

Para cambiar la captura del producto:
- `assets/products/nexus-enterprise-dashboard.webp`
